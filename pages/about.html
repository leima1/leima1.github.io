I am a neuroscience researcher and physical therapist dedicated to understanding how the brain overcomes sensory and motor deficits. I use virtual reality, motion capture, and surface and intracranial EEG recordings to investigate the neural mechanisms underlying balance, gait, and motor control.

My ultimate goal is to bridge the gap between clinical practice and basic science, translating laboratory discoveries into real-world interventions.

Research Focus

  • Neural correlates of gait and balance in Parkinson's disease
  • Sensory integration and attentional control for gait and balance
  • Virtual reality as an assessment and rehabilitation tool
  • Cortical dynamics (EEG, iEEG) during naturalistic movement
